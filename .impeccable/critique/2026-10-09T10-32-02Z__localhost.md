Design specificity: model concepts, source-workspace connections and lifecycle badges belong to a governed knowledge product. The composition resembles a generic card catalog; repeated icons and four metric tiles crowd out differences between models. Preserve the dark theme and semantic accent roles while improving hierarchy.

Priority suggestions:

1. [P1] Expose and organize filtering. At the 1038 x 668 viewport with a 256px rail, tabs beyond Automatic are partially or fully outside the toolbar. Seven tabs mix scope, construction method, record presence, health and archive state. Keep All / Shared with me as scope tabs. Put other dimensions into labeled filter controls with active-filter chips. Add result count and a sort control for supported fields. Suggested command: clarify.
2. [P2] Compress cards and clarify primary action. Sixteen cards repeat a large model icon, a colored top rule, four metric tiles and multiple actions. Use name, two-line purpose, lifecycle status, a compact secondary metric line, and one clear Open action. Put Rename, Share, Copy and Delete into an accessible overflow menu. Consider a compact list option for frequent operators. Surface updated/owner information only where available and authorized. Suggested command: distill.
3. [P1] Make knowledge selection easier to understand. The almost full-height creation dialog includes name, purpose and a scrollable list of 52 workspaces, with 20 shared before 32 personal. Label the workspace search accessibly, group source scopes, show a persistent selected-count/summary, and state whether connection is optional or required according to actual behavior. Keep the useful business-purpose question and the existing no-copy/no-move explanation. Suggested command: onboard.
4. [P2] Prevent the assistant from covering model actions. The floating Yellowmind control overlaps the lower-right card action area at the tested viewport. Reserve space or relocate/collapse it within the app shell. Suggested command: polish.

Minor observations: distinguish construction method (Designed / Automatic), publication status (Draft / Published), and data readiness (records) rather than implying they mean the same thing. Explain what 0 records means; do not infer that a published model is broken. Fix visible singular/plural grammar such as 1 workspaces and 1 records. Do not change stored model names or descriptions as part of visual refinement.

Personas: frequent operators scan too much repeated card chrome to reopen known work; first-time creators must interpret the relationship between workspace selection and the model; keyboard/screen-reader users need visible accessible source-search labels and discoverable filters. Complete keyboard/a11y testing was not performed.

Cognitive load: seven mutually exclusive tabs mix several dimensions; card actions repeat sixteen times; a long source list makes creation feel heavier. Existing card/source grouping and the reassuring purpose copy help.

Emotional journey: clear purpose and Create action orient the user; similar cards and a long source list create uncertainty; Cancel and Clear filters provide reliable exits. Creation completion and failure recovery were not tested.
