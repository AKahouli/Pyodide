# Platform Overview

- **Scope and mode:** `/platform`, Operate with an orientation-first opening. It serves both first-session understanding and repeat-visit direct access without becoming the future personalized User Workbench.
- **Audience and job:** Business users arrive with a goal, not product taxonomy. YellowStorm recommends a capability chain, keeps conversation as the primary starting point, and provides compact direct launchers for familiar users.
- **Proof and content:** Auditability is the primary confidence mechanism. Reliability evidence is described conditionally and includes available evaluation confidence plus supported, partially supported, unsupported, or contradicted claims. Connected apps appear only as delegated tool authorization; App Marketplace contains apps published from conversations.
- **Direction and memorable moment:** A split first viewport pairs the business-goal statement with four outcome routes. A high-contrast evidence ledger immediately demonstrates inspectable confidence, followed by one connected journey from conversation through context, agents, playbooks, and governance. Worky is a floating super-agent/digital-twin launcher that guides the next step.
- **Constraints:** Preserve semantic themes, EN/FR localization, verified routes, governance and semantic-model permissions, and administrator-only controls. Do not fabricate recent work, embed a fake Worky chat, promise a reliability score for every answer, or imply that a standalone app builder exists.
